# Projects

After Level 0 you can build something of your own. Start small, ship it, then show it in the chat.

- A Telegram bot that answers a question of your choice.
- A script that keeps your notes organised.
- A small website published on GitHub Pages.
- A command line tool that solves a problem you actually have.

> **Note.** Levels 1 and 2 are in progress. They will cover Bash, processes, networking, Docker and CI.
